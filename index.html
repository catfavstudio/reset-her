Help Guide

💌 Daily Prompt

Every day you get one affirmation and one mindset question. Write your answer in the box. This helps you notice your thoughts and slowly build new, kinder ones.

🌤️ Mood Check In

Tap the face that matches how you feel. Do this once a day. Over time you will see your mood history and start to notice patterns.

✅ Habit Tracker

Add habits you want to build. Tap the circle each day you complete them. Your streak counts how many days in a row you have kept a habit going.

🪞 Identity Journal

Each day you get a new "I am becoming" statement. Write about it. This is how you practice becoming the woman you wrote about in your Future Her letter.

💌 Future Her

The letter you wrote before Day 1 is locked away safely. On Day 90 it unlocks so you can read it and see how far you have come.

💾 Backing Up Your Data

Everything you write is saved only on this device, in this browser. It is never sent to us or anyone else. This means if you clear your browser data or switch devices, your progress can be lost. Tap "Back up now" often. It saves a small file to your device. Keep that file safe. You can use "Restore" in Settings to bring your data back if anything happens.

🔑 Your Unlock Code

Your unlock code came from your Gumroad receipt. Once you enter it correctly one time, this device stays unlocked forever. If you use a new phone or computer, you will need to enter the same code there too.

🌸 Identity Reset Challenge

Pick challenges from ten categories that fit your life. Check them off each day. This is your own custom challenge, not a strict set of rules. Do as many or as few as feel right.

🙏 Gratitude Log

Write three good things from your day. Even small things count. This helps train your mind to notice the good.

🎯 Vision Board

Write down goals and things you want in your life. Come back and read them any time you need a reminder of why you started.

💗 Self Care Checklist

A simple daily list of ways to care for yourself. Check off what you did today.

📅 Weekly Review

Once a week, look back and write about what went well, what was hard, and what you want to do differently. This helps you learn from each week instead of just moving past it.

🏆 Milestones

Every 10 days you show up and use the app, you unlock a new milestone. This is a way to see your consistency build over time.
